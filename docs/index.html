Privacy Policy

Application: My Organiser (com.ai2099model.myorganiser)  |  Effective Date: September 9, 2026  |  Applicability: Global, United States (CCPA/CPRA) & European Union / UK (GDPR)

Privacy-First & Offline-First: My Organiser stores your personal tasks, daily schedules, reminders, ledger records, contact notes, and secure biometric vault documents locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

1. Overview & Local Data Architecture

We believe your personal schedule and confidential files should remain completely in your control. The application is built around local on-device sandboxed storage using encrypted database technology provided by the Android OS.

  • Local Data Storage: All calendar entries, audio reminders, contact records, ledger transactions, and documents are stored locally within the application sandbox.
  • No Telemetry Tracking: We do not track or profile user behavior across other apps or websites.

2. European Union & UK Compliance (GDPR & UK GDPR)

EU GDPR & UK Data Protection

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), European Union, or United Kingdom, the following terms govern our data handling under Regulation (EU) 2016/679:

A. Legal Bases for Processing (Article 6)

  • Contractual Performance: Processing required to provide core scheduling and document storage features.
  • Consent: Explicit user consent granted for camera, microphone speech-to-text, and notifications. Consent can be revoked anytime via device settings.
  • Legitimate Interests: Securing data on your device and maintaining offline database integrity.

B. Your Data Subject Rights (Articles 15–22)

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): View all stored records directly in the app.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Edit and update your data at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Permanently delete items, clear app data, or uninstall the app to erase all records.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Export local archives using the backup manager.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection (Arts. 18 & 21): Exercise full control over local processing.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your national Supervisory Authority (e.g., CNIL, BfDI, ICO).

3. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA & State Laws)

California CCPA/CPRA & US State Regulations

For residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other US states:

  • "Do Not Sell or Share My Information": We do NOT sell your personal data or share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to Know & Delete: Request disclosure or complete deletion of all records via the app interface or app storage settings.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We never discriminate against any user for exercising their privacy rights.
  • Children's Privacy (COPPA): The application does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age.

4. Hardware Permissions & Device Security

  • Biometric Unlock: Secured through Android's hardware BiometricPrompt framework. Biometric templates never leave your device hardware.
  • Camera & Media: Used solely when capturing or attaching photos or scanning QR codes within the app.
  • Speech Recognition: Processes audio solely on-device during active speech recognition input.
  • AI Image Studio: Optional user creative prompts sent securely via HTTPS/TLS 1.3 without personal identifiers.

5. Data Retention & Instant Deletion

Your data remains stored on your device only for as long as you keep the application installed. You can delete individual items instantly within the app, export a full backup, or wipe all application data via Android Settings > Apps > My Organiser > Clear Storage.
