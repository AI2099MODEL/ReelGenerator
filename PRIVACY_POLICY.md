# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 28, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community by using our application. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
Our App is designed with a privacy-first, offline-first approach. 
- **Local Storage:** All your personal data—including tasks, diary entries, health logs, expense records, and vault documents—are stored **locally** on your device's internal storage using a local database. We do not transmit, collect, or store this data on our servers.
- **Biometric Authentication:** The App utilizes your device's built-in biometric authentication (e.g., fingerprint or face unlock) to secure your "My Vault" section. Biometric data never leaves your device and is processed entirely by your device's operating system.
- **Location Data:** The App may request access to your device's location to provide location-tagging features (e.g., tagging a diary entry or event). This location data is processed locally and saved only to your device. We do not track or upload your location.
- **Camera Access:** The App requests camera access to allow you to take photos or scan QR codes within the App. Images captured are processed and stored locally on your device.

### 2. Third-Party Services and APIs
To provide certain features, the App communicates with third-party Application Programming Interfaces (APIs).
- **AI Image Generation (Pollinations AI):** When you use the "Image Studio" feature to generate images, the text prompt you enter is sent securely over the internet to the Pollinations AI API. No personal identifiers are attached to these requests. Please refer to [Pollinations.ai's Privacy Policy](https://pollinations.ai) for more information on how they handle requests.

### 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
Because your data is stored locally on your device, **we do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to others.** We do not have access to your locally stored data.

### 4. Data Security
We implement reasonable security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your data is restricted to the App's secure sandbox environment provided by the Android Operating System. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your device secure and utilizing the App's built-in security features, such as biometric locks.

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Your data is retained on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. You can delete all your data at any time by:
- Deleting individual items within the App.
- Clearing the App's data in your device's Settings.
- Uninstalling the App.

### 6. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13, as we do not collect personal data on our servers at all.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
